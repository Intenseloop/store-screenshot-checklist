## Screenshots

- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, with no alpha channel.
- The shortest side is at least 320 px and the longest side at most 3840 px.
- The longest side can't be more than twice the shortest side.
- At least 2 screenshots in total, and up to 8 per device type.

Each device type (phone, 7-inch tablet, 10-inch tablet, Chromebook, Android TV, Wear OS, cars, Android XR) has its own section under Graphics in Play Console.

## Feature graphic

The feature graphic is a fixed **1024 × 500 px**, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha. It's required, and it shows at the top of the listing in some places, so keep text away from the edges. The [feature graphic guide](https://appscreen.pro/blog/google-play-feature-graphic) covers where it appears and what gets it cut.

## Icon

The store icon is **512 × 512 px**, a 32-bit PNG of at most 1024 KB.

More detail, including the sizes Google recommends to be eligible for featured placements, is in [Google Play screenshot and feature graphic sizes](https://appscreen.pro/blog/google-play-screenshot-sizes). Google's source is Play Console Help's [preview assets](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151) page.
