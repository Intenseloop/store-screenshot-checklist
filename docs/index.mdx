title: Store Screenshot Checklist
description: What to check before you upload screenshots to App Store Connect or Google Play Console.
---

Screenshots are the part of a store listing people look at before they read a word. Both stores also reject files for small reasons: a stray alpha channel, a size one pixel off, a ratio that's too tall. This checklist collects the rules in one place so you can catch those before the upload fails.

## Before you start

- Decide which devices you ship on. Each one needs its own set: iPhone and iPad on Apple's side, phones and tablets (plus Chromebook, TV, Wear OS and cars if you support them) on Google's.
- Export flat images. Both stores refuse transparency, so save PNGs without an alpha channel or use JPEG.
- Keep the first two or three slides for your main promise. They're the ones shown in search results and on the first screen of the listing.
- Write captions short enough to read on a phone at arm's length.

## The pages here

The numbers come from Apple's and Google's own documentation as summarized in the [AppScreen guides](https://appscreen.pro/blog), checked in November 2026. Store rules change when new devices ship, so look at the official pages before a big release.
