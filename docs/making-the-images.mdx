## In a design tool

Figma, Sketch or any editor works: make one frame per store size, place your captures in device mockups, add captions and export each frame. It's flexible, but every size and every language means another set of frames to keep in sync. [This walkthrough](https://appscreen.pro/blog/app-store-screenshots-in-figma) covers the Figma route step by step.

## In the browser with AppScreen

[AppScreen](https://appscreen.pro) is a browser editor made for this job. You drop in your app captures, place them in iPhone or Android phone frames, write captions over one wide panorama canvas, and export a PNG per slide at the store's size. It's free, needs no account and saves your work in your browser.

## Small free tools

A few single-purpose helpers that run in the browser:

- [App Store screenshot resizer](https://appscreen.pro/tools/app-store-screenshot-resizer): checks the sizes you have and resizes to the ones App Store Connect takes, removing transparency on the way.
- [Panorama splitter](https://appscreen.pro/tools/panorama-splitter): cuts one wide image into consecutive screenshots.
- [Google Play feature graphic generator](https://appscreen.pro/tools/feature-graphic-generator): lays out a 1024 × 500 graphic.

## Last check before uploading

- Right size for each upload box, and no alpha channel.
- The first slides carry the main message.
- Captions readable on a phone.
- Every language you list has its own set, or you've accepted the default for the rest.
